# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

For deaf and hard-of-hearing people who need to communicate with hearing people who do not know sign language, **Traduce Señas** is a mobile sign language translation application that recognizes signs through the device's camera and translates them into text or audio in real time.

Unlike relying exclusively on professional interpreters or alternative communication methods, our product provides an accessible technological alternative designed to facilitate communication in everyday situations.

## Product Goal

The main goal of Traduce Señas is to reduce communication barriers between deaf or hard-of-hearing people and hearing people by providing a simple and accessible tool for real-time sign recognition and translation.

## Target Users

- Deaf or hard-of-hearing people who use sign language.
- Hearing people who need to communicate with sign language users.
- Administrators responsible for supervising and managing the system.

## Core Value

Traduce Señas provides value by:

- Facilitating communication between deaf and hearing people.
- Recognizing signs through a device camera.
- Translating recognized signs into text and audio.
- Providing an accessible and intuitive mobile experience.
- Supporting communication in everyday situations.
- Reducing partial dependence on the availability of professional interpreters.

## Product Scope

The current product focuses on:

- Sign recognition using the device camera.
- Real-time translation into text and audio.
- User accounts and authentication.
- Translation history.
- Sign language alphabet.
- User preferences such as language and theme.
- Notifications within the application.
- Administrative usage statistics.

## What We Are Not Building in This Cycle

The current scope does not include:

- Reverse translation from text or voice into signs.
- A 3D sign language avatar.
- The complete Colombian Sign Language vocabulary.
- Full offline functionality.

These limitations define the boundaries of the current product and help keep development focused on the core problem: facilitating communication through real-time sign recognition and translation.
